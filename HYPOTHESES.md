| H3 | History is needed: k=1 can't infer the hidden movement phase (the player moves every 2nd tick with buffered input), so it plateaus. k≥2 nearly solves stage 1. Dinos need 2–4 frames for momentum. | Sweep k ∈ {1,2,3,4}. Compare changed-cell accuracy on stage 1 and dino-cell accuracy on stage 3. *(Revised 2026-10-05 after the harness finding.)* Early hint: a hand-written 2-frame rule beats the 1-frame rule on stage 1, 68.6% vs 40.1% dynamic accuracy. **Result 2026-10-05: k=1 peaked at 22.6% dynamic acc; k=2 reached 65.5% and was still rising.** **k=4 (3 epochs): 71.6%, beats k=2 (65.9%) and the hand rule (68.6%); water errors −40%.** | 🟢 supported (k=1 vs 2) |
