Arrow keys to move. You are the white ①. Eat dinos of your level or lower, avoid bigger ones and lava.
The net only starts from four frames of a real game. After that everything is its imagination: terrain that scrolls into view, lava spreading, where every dino goes, whether you get eaten. It will drift from the real game, and that's the point. The net predicts every change; a little code applies them (it moves each dino by the net's predicted move, rolls dice for lava and dino moves, and only lets new dinos appear at the edge the view just revealed).
